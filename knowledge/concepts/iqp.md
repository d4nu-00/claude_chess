---
title: Isolated Queen's Pawn (IQP)
tags: [iqp, weak-pawns]
---
## Summary
- IQP owner: dynamic middlegame — keep pieces on, use e5/c5 (or e4/c4) outposts, rook lifts, kingside attack.
- The d4-d5 (or ...d5-d4) break frees the position; time it when pieces are ready.
- Defender: blockade the square in front of the pawn (ideally with a knight), trade minor pieces.
- Every trade favours the defender; in the endgame the IQP is a pure weakness.

## Details
Typical from QGA, Tarrasch, Caro-Kann Panov, Nimzo e3 lines. Owner's knights on e5/c3, bishop on the b1-h7 diagonal, queen+bishop battery at h7. Defender: Nd5 blockade (or Nd4 vs Black IQP), pressure with Rd8/Qd-file, trade the light-squared bishop that points at the king.
