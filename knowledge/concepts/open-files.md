## Summary
- Occupy open files with rooks (doubling makes them stronger); contest the enemy's file.
- A rook on the 7th (2nd) rank attacks pawns and confines the enemy king.
- Half-open files are targets: pressure the backward/isolated pawn at their end.
- The side with a file should look for an entry square on the 7th or 8th rank.
