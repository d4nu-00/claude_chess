## Summary
- Rook vs minor piece: the rook side wants open files and simplification toward an endgame.
- The minor-piece side wants outposts, pawns for compensation, and a closed position.
- Two rooks vs queen, rook vs two minors: coordination and king safety decide.
- Positional exchange sacrifices (…Rxc3) for structure or initiative are common and sound.
