## Summary
- Count attackers vs defenders near the king; three or more attacking pieces signal a real attack.
- Missing shield pawns and open files near the king invite sacrifices (Bxh7+, Nxf7, Rxg7).
- Defend by trading attacking pieces, especially the queen; do not push shield pawns without reason.
- A king stuck in the centre with open files is a tactical liability — castle or trade queens.
