## Summary
- Black: pawns a6/b6/d6/e6, pieces compact behind; waits for ...b5 or ...d5 break.
- White: space and restraint — prevent the breaks; don't overextend with g4/f4 unless prepared.
- The ...d5 break usually opens lines when White's pieces are misplaced — count tactics before it.
- Trades generally help White (less space needed); Black keeps pieces to keep tension.
