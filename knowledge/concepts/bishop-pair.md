## Summary
- Two bishops are worth ~half a pawn extra in open positions; open the position with pawn breaks.
- Side with the pair: avoid trading one bishop cheaply, restrict enemy knights by pushing pawns.
- Opponent: keep the position closed, establish knight outposts, trade one bishop.
- Opposite-coloured bishops: drawish endgames, but in the middlegame they favour the attacker.
