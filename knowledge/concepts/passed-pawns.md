## Summary
- Passed pawns must be pushed — but only with support; a blockaded passer is worthless.
- Blockade an enemy passer with a knight (ideal blockader) directly in front of it.
- Protected and connected passers are decisive in endgames; trade pieces to exploit them.
- Rooks belong behind passed pawns, both for supporting and stopping them.
