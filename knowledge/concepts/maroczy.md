## Summary
- White pawns c4+e4 control d5 and restrict ...b5/...d5 breaks: space advantage.
- White: keep the bind, avoid piece trades that free Black, play Rc1/b4/c5 on the queenside.
- Black: trade pieces (esp. dark bishops), use ...a5/Nd7-c5 manoeuvres, look for ...b5 or ...f5.
- A premature ...d5 or ...b5 often fails tactically; prepare it fully.
