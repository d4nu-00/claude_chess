## Summary
- White d5+e4 vs Black d6+e5: the centre is closed, play on the wings where your pawns point.
- White: queenside expansion b4, c5, Nd2-c4, break on c7/d6.
- Black: kingside storm ...Ne8/...Nh5, ...f5-f4, ...g5-g4 against White's king.
- It is a race: every tempo counts; do not open the side where you are slower.
