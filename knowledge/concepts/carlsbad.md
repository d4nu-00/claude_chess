## Summary
- QGD Exchange structure: White d4/e3 vs Black c6/d5, c-file half-open for White, e-file for Black.
- White: minority attack b4-b5xc6 leaving Black a weak c-pawn; or f3 and e4 central break.
- Black: kingside piece play (Ne4, ...f5, rook lift via e6), exchange light-squared bishops.
- Black should not answer the minority attack passively; ...b5 or ...a6/...b5 can halt it.
