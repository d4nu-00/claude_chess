## Summary
- Count: the square of the pawn rule; the king must catch an unsupported passer inside its square.
- Opposition: kings facing with one square between — the side NOT to move holds it.
- Key squares: get the king to the squares two ranks in front of the pawn to force promotion.
- Outside passed pawns decoy the enemy king; triangulation loses a tempo to win the opposition.
