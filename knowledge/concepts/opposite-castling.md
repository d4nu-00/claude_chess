## Summary
- Kings on opposite wings: the race of pawn storms decides; material matters less than tempo.
- Push the pawns in front of the enemy king; open files toward it; sacrifices to open lines are typical.
- Don't waste tempi on defence unless the threat is immediate; a counterattack is often the best defence.
- Central control slows both attacks — whoever controls the centre can switch sides.
