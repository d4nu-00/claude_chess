## Summary
- Isolated, doubled and backward pawns are long-term targets: fix them, then attack with pieces.
- The square in front of an isolated/backward pawn is an ideal outpost for the opponent.
- Pawn majorities: the side with a healthy majority aims to create a passed pawn in the endgame.
- Fewer pawn islands is usually better.
