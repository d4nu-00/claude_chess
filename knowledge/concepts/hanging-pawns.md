## Summary
- Two side-by-side pawns (c+d) with no neighbours: strong while abreast, weak once one is forced forward.
- Owner: control the squares in front, prepare d5/c5 with tempo, use half-open b- and e-files.
- Attacker: lever with ...b5/...e5 style pawn breaks and pile pieces on c4/d4 to force an advance.
- After one advances, the other becomes backward or isolated — blockade the hole.

## Details
Often arise from QGD Tartakower and Queen's Indian lines.
