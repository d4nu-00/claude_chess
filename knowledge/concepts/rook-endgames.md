## Summary
- Rooks belong behind passed pawns (yours or the enemy's); activity beats material.
- Philidor defence (R+P vs R): keep rook on 6th rank until the pawn advances, then check from behind.
- Lucena (pawn on 7th, king in front): build a bridge — rook to 4th rank shields checks.
- Cut the enemy king off by files; an active king and rook are often worth a pawn.
