## Summary
- An outpost is a square supported by your pawn that no enemy pawn can attack.
- Knights on 5th/6th-rank outposts are often stronger than rooks; route them there (Nd2-f1-e3-d5).
- Remove the defender that could trade off your outpost piece.
- Avoid pawn moves that create outposts for the opponent.
