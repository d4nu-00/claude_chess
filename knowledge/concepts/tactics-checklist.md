## Summary
- Before every move: checks, captures, threats — for BOTH sides.
- Loose pieces drop off: look for double attacks on undefended pieces (forks, skewers).
- Pinned pieces are not real defenders; overloaded defenders cannot guard two things.
- After choosing a move, ask: what is the opponent's most forcing reply?
