## Summary
- White d5 vs Black c5+d6: White has central majority, Black a queenside majority and the long diagonal.
- White: e4-e5 break, restrain ...b5 (a4), pressure d6.
- Black: ...a6/...b5 expansion, pieces on e-file, ...Ne5/...Nc5, ...f5 lever.
- The e4 square and White's e-pawn advance are the critical theme.
