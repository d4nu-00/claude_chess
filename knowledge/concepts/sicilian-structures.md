## Summary
- Open Sicilian: White has more space and development, Black has the half-open c-file and central pawn majority.
- Scheveningen (d6+e6): White pawn storm g4-g5 or f4-f5; Black ...d5 or ...b5-b4 counterplay.
- Najdorf/Boleslavsky (d6+e5): the d5 square is the key — White occupies it, Black fights with ...d5.
- Opposite-side castling races are common: count tempi of pawn storms.
