## Summary
- Develop knights and bishops quickly, castle early, connect rooks.
- Fight for the centre with pawns and pieces; don't move the same piece twice without reason.
- Don't grab pawns (especially with the queen) at the cost of development against a developed opponent.
- A lead in development must be used by opening lines before the opponent catches up.
