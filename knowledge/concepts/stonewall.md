## Summary
- Pawns d4/e3/f4 (or d5/e6/f5) give a firm outpost on e5 (e4) but a hole on e4 (e5).
- Owner: knight to the outpost, kingside attack with g4/h4 or rook lift, keep the bad bishop active.
- Opponent: occupy the hole, trade the good dark-squared bishop, then queenside play.
- Beware the light-squared (or dark) bishop locked behind own pawns.
