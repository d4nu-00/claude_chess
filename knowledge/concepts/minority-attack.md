## Summary
- Advance fewer pawns (b- and a-pawns) against a majority to create a weak pawn (usually c6).
- Rooks go behind: Rb1/Rc1; Qb3 and pressure on c6 after bxc6.
- Works best when the defender lacks active counterplay on the other wing.
- Defender: meet b5 with ...c5 only if d5 is not then isolated; or counterattack on the kingside.
