## Summary
- White d4-e5 vs Black d5-e6: White has kingside space, Black attacks the chain base.
- Black: ...c5 hitting d4, ...Qb6, ...Nc6, later ...f6 undermining e5.
- White: keep d4 supported (c3, Nf3), then f4-f5 or queenside space; bad French bishop is Black's worry.
- Trading Black's light-squared bishop (...Bd7-b5 or ...b6/...Ba6) improves Black.
