claude_chess · architecture

Claude as a chess engine

Claude supplies the judgement a classical engine gets from its move ordering and evaluation function. Python supplies the parts an LLM is bad at: exact board state, legality, and tree bookkeeping. The experiment removes one wire, the context block, and measures what changes.

engine-ctx vs engine-noctx answers “does context help?”. engine-noctx vs naive answers “does search help?”. Every game gets analysed by Stockfish afterwards for average centipawn loss (ACPL), blunders and illegal-move rate, so small samples still give per-move signal beyond win/loss.

Policy

Illegal moves

Illegal or unparseable candidates are dropped and logged. If no candidate is legal, Claude is re-asked with the specific error and the full legal list, up to three times. After that the configured policy applies. The default plays a random legal move and flags it, so games finish and the failure still counts in the metrics. The forfeit policy, like a UCI engine sending an illegal move in engine tournaments, scores it as a loss.
